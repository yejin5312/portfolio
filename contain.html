CONTAIN | Vase

Year 2026
Duration 15 weeks
Personal Project
Glass, Solid Beech / 260*280*30

CONTAIN is a series of glass vases inspired by the layered forms, patterns, and textures of textiles. Each vase translates the soft and fluid qualities of fabric into glass through fusing and slumping techniques, creating a contrast between a rigid material and a familiar, tactile surface.

#Pattern Development#Material Exploration

Textiles are familiar materials found throughout our everyday lives, wrapping, covering, and shaping the spaces around us. They are soft and flexible, naturally folding, overlapping, and flowing with movement. These changing forms reveal patterns and textures that give each textile its own character. These qualities allow textiles to create a sense of softness and warmth in our everyday surroundings.

CONTAIN brings the soft, layered qualities of textile into glass, reinterpreting fabric’s patterns, transparency, and flowing forms through a rigid material. The two vases explore this relationship through check and stripe patterns, using curved structures and transparent elements to let the flower and pattern become part of one another.
