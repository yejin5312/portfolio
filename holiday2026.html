HOLIDAY 2026 | Ornament

Year 2026
Duration 15 weeks
Personal Project
Glass / 150*150*15

HOLIDAY 2026 is a collection of glass Christmas ornaments inspired by familiar symbols and imagery of the holiday season. Using plaster casting and glass kiln-forming techniques, each ornament transforms these familiar forms into small glass objects, capturing the warmth and charm of Christmas.

#Holiday Objects#Kiln Forming

As Christmas approaches, everyone finds their own way to welcome the season. Some cut, fold, and glue pieces of paper to make ornaments by hand, while others choose decorations to bring a little Christmas magic into their homes. Whether made, collected, or carefully chosen, these small preparations become part of the joy of waiting for Christmas.

HOLIDAY 2026 takes inspiration from these personal ways of preparing for Christmas. The collection translates familiar holiday symbols into glass ornaments, celebrating the small rituals, creative expressions, and anticipation that make the season special.
